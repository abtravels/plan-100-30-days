# plan-100-30-days

A plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in
30 days: book sales meetings for small SaaS founders — 3 free meetings to
start, then $60 per meeting held or $1,500 a month per client.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the plan text to
`/output/entry.txt`. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138)
"The plan make.fun will carry out in public".
